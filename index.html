.RAW, real, and authentic

At The Color Lab, we take a step away from the modern traditions of digital color to bring your frames to life. Our unorthodox, artistic workflows give clients' films a look you cannot get anywhere else. From short films, to features, to documentaries, no matter what software you are cutting in, we can help you achieve the look you want.

Our Workflows

We offer a variety of workflows to suit your preferences, as well as different grading tiers depending on what you are looking for.

DaVinci Resolve

Most of our grades are done in DaVinci Resolve, which is the industry standard for color grading. We can grade your film in a variety of ways, from a simple LUT application to a full color grade with multiple passes and adjustments. This is preferable for clients who are looking for a more cinematic look, or who want to have more control over the final look of their film. Footage from any other software can be pushed to DaVinci Resolve, with some caveats. All of our Final Cut grades are done in DaVinci. Learn more here!

Premiere Pro

If you are cutting in Premiere Pro, we can grade your film in Premiere Pro as well. This is preferable for clients who are looking for a more natural look or more minimal adjustments. We can also grade your film in Premiere Pro if you are looking for a more cinematic look, but we recommend using DaVinci Resolve for the best results. Learn more here!
